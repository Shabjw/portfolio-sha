# Curiosity Map assets

Generated with the built-in image generation tool for this Hero. Each scene is a separate transparent bitmap, not a static interface screenshot. The final files are resized, alpha-trimmed and compressed as WebP. Interface copy, plus buttons, notes and the connecting thread are rendered separately in HTML/CSS/SVG.

## Prompt set

- **seoul.webp:** Isolated horizontal editorial travel collage on transparent background. A four-button brushed-metal elevator panel with one softly glowing amber button beside an ivory Seoul postcard with N Seoul Tower and a faded blue ticket. Fine navy ink, tactile watercolor paper, warm cream, dusk blue and terracotta. No interface controls or explanatory text baked into the image.
- **skinventory.webp:** Isolated horizontal editorial collage of three unbranded skincare containers: amber dropper, pale sage pump, ivory jar. A small inventory checklist and blue receipt behind them. Fine ink and tactile watercolor, warm afternoon light, transparent surrounding space, no brand or interface controls.
- **badminton.webp:** Isolated navy and ivory badminton racket with detailed string mesh, a cream feather shuttlecock and a torn dusty-blue court sketch. Warm ink-and-watercolor notebook style, transparent surrounding space. Trajectory and labels are supplied by the website.
- **dinosaur.webp:** One tiny friendly sage-green long-neck dinosaur, looking back, with a fine navy pencil outline, watercolor texture and a terracotta cheek. Transparent background; no frame or scenery.
- **travel.webp:** Optimised transparent crop of the existing `image/worldmap.png`; not newly generated.

## Refinement assets

Generated with the built-in image tool using the existing illustrations as style references. Alpha-trimmed, resized and compressed to WebP for the website; originals retained in the generated-images folder.

- **coffee-travel-v2.webp:** One cohesive transparent editorial ink-and-watercolour collage: ivory cappuccino cup and saucer, folded linen napkin, faded coastal-city postcard and narrow blue travel ticket. Match the Seoul/skincare scene's warm light, tactile paper, navy outlines and restrained colour palette. No labels, map, logos or interface elements.
- **shuttlecock.webp:** Extract the cream feather shuttlecock style from the badminton reference into a separate transparent illustration. Preserve the textured feather detail, ivory cork and navy band; remove the racket, court, paper and all other objects. This independent asset follows a curved SVG motion path without cutting into the original image.
- **coffee-cards-v3.webp:** Built-in image edit of the coffee/travel scene, with Seoul as a style reference. Preserve the ivory ceramic cappuccino, saucer, linen napkin, realistic ink-and-watercolour texture and golden-hour light. Remove all travel imagery and flowers; replace them with exactly two small ivory playing cards, terracotta heart and navy club, tucked naturally beside the cup. Transparent background, complete objects, soft shadows, no logos, maps or new story elements. Optimised to an alpha-trimmed 840px WebP; previous illustration retained unchanged.
